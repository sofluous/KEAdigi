How to Play

Press keys on your real keyboard to light up the matching keys here.

  • Tap on-screen keys to show or hide their labels.
  • Use the highlight buttons to reveal groups like function, modifier, or control keys.
  • Switch between fit-to-view and scale mode depending on the screen size you need.
