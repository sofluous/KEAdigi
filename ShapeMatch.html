How to Play

Match each piece to a target with the same shape, and later the same color too.

  • Drag a piece onto a matching target, or tap a piece and then a target.
  • When typing mode is active, press a target number and a piece letter to place it.
  • Build combos by making quick correct matches before the combo bar runs out.
